# Ordinus product context

## Register

product

## Users

People planning Gridfinity storage in physical drawers. The README documents desktop, tablet, and mobile use. The Simple workflow serves novices arranging everyday items such as silverware; Advanced serves users who customize generator inputs and reference images.

## Product Purpose

Plan a drawer, configure printable bins, save a layout, and produce a bill of materials and printable output. Geometry and exported output must correspond to the physical grid.

## Brand Personality

Practical, precise, approachable. Preserve the existing application vocabulary and components. No separate marketing identity is introduced by the Simple workflow.

## Anti-references

Avoid requiring novices to browse many footprint variants or understand raw generator parameters. Avoid decorative redesigns of established workspace controls.

## Design Principles

- Show the physical footprint before configuration details.
- Expose friendly choices over one maintained parametric model.
- Keep advanced controls available without crowding the novice workflow.
- Validate dimensions and placement before changing the layout.
- Preserve the same geometry through saving and printing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing README promises mouse and touch workflows. New controls use native labels, visible focus, keyboard input, and text explaining invalid states. No formal conformance claim is made.
